The problem

Small businesses needed a faster way to manage customers, products, invoices, sales, expenses and follow-up without splitting everyday operations across disconnected tools.

Constraints

  • Multiple businesses had to share one platform without mixing their information.
  • Mobile flows had to remain practical at the point of sale.
  • Invoice printing had to work with Bluetooth ESC/POS devices in the field.
  • The service needed a maintainable AWS/Linux operating context.

My responsibility

I contributed across the product: Flutter mobile workflows, REST backend, data layer, integrations, deployment and technical support.

Architecture

Flutter mobile client → REST API → AdonisJS / Node.js services → MySQL, deployed on AWS/Linux behind Nginx. Authentication and tenant context sit at the center of each business operation.

Flutter appREST APIAdonisJSMySQL

Key engineering decisions

Logical tenant isolation

Each operation resolves within the correct business context, allowing several businesses to use one platform while keeping their data boundaries explicit.

REST boundary for the mobile client

A stable API contract keeps business rules centralized and lets the mobile experience evolve without directly coupling to data storage.

Direct Bluetooth ESC/POS printing

Printing stays inside the point-of-sale workflow instead of depending on a desktop intermediary.

AWS/Linux with Nginx

A lightweight, controllable deployment setup matched the operational needs of the product.

What I built

  • Customers, products, sales, invoice and expense workflows.
  • Roles, employees, suppliers and business summaries.
  • Bluetooth invoice printing and REST endpoints consumed by the mobile app.

Hard problems

Key challenges included tenant-aware data access, reliable mobile/API synchronization, Bluetooth printing behaviour and operational differences between staging and production.

Quality & testing

Validation relied on endpoint integration, product use and production support. Exact automated coverage is intentionally not estimated here.

Deployment & operations

AWS/Linux infrastructure with Nginx, configuration practices, backups and environment separation supported the operational workflow.

Results

200+ invoices per month are supported in production. The invoice-printing flow moved from approximately three minutes to under thirty seconds, and more than ten REST endpoints were integrated into the mobile experience.

What I would improve today

  • Add stronger automated coverage around tenant boundaries and printing failures.
  • Make observability and incident history visible to the team.
  • Document deployment and backup runbooks as first-class product artifacts.
